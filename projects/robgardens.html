Client site

Rob's Gardens

A marketing website for Rob's Gardens, a garden and maintenance business on Sydney's Northern Beaches, built to win local search and turn visitors into quote requests.

ReactViteLocal SEO
The problem

Rob's Gardens is a hands-on garden and maintenance business on Sydney's Northern Beaches. It needed a website that reflected the quality of the work, made the range of services clear, and helped local customers find it and get in touch.

What was built

A fast, responsive single-page site with a section for each service — tree and palm work, hedging, lawns, and mulching — laid out in an editorial style with real photos of Rob and the fleet. Every page routes visitors toward a quote or a phone call, and the site is structured so it ranks well for the suburbs Rob actually covers.

The outcome

Rob's Gardens has a professional web presence that matches the standard of the work and gives local customers a clear, direct path to book. Built with React and Vite.
