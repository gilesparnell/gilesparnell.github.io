Companies.

Firms that sell training, funding or signals to retail traders. Someone asks me to look at one, so I read the site properly and check it against the public registers — then write down what I found.

ⓘ

My own notes on publicly available information. General information, not financial advice, and not a recommendation to use or avoid any firm. Verify everything independently before committing money.

Each write-up follows the same shape: what's actually on offer, stripped of marketing; where it's genuinely strong; where it should give you pause; and a short list of questions to ask before any money moves. Affiliate review sites are excluded on purpose — most earn a commission on sign-ups. Where I've drawn an inference rather than verified a fact, I say so.
